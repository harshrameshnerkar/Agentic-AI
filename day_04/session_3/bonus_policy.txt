# Enterprise Compensation & Performance Bonus Policy (Fiscal Year 2026)
Document ID: POL-HR-2026-Q3
Classification: Internal Corporate Policy
Effective Date: July 1, 2026

## 1. Overview
This policy outlines the calculation methodology for quarterly discretionary performance bonuses across enterprise departments. Bonuses are calculated as a fixed percentage of annual base salary.

## 2. Departmental Bonus Percentages

### A. Engineering Department
- Executive / VP Level: 25% (multiplier: 0.25)
- Principal / Staff Engineers: 20% (multiplier: 0.20)
- Senior Engineers (Senior Software Engineer, Senior DevOps Engineer, Senior SRE): 12% (multiplier: 0.12)
- Software Engineer II / Mid-Level: 8% (multiplier: 0.08)
- Associate / Junior Engineer: 5% (multiplier: 0.05)

### B. Data Science & AI Department
- Director / Lead Data Scientist: 20% (multiplier: 0.20)
- Senior Data Scientist / ML Engineer: 15% (multiplier: 0.15)
- Data Analyst / Associate: 10% (multiplier: 0.10)

### C. Product Management & Design
- VP of Product: 22% (multiplier: 0.22)
- Senior Product Manager: 14% (multiplier: 0.14)
- Product Designer: 10% (multiplier: 0.10)

### D. Security & Compliance
- Chief Information Security Officer (CISO): 25% (multiplier: 0.25)
- Senior Security Engineer / Cloud Architect: 16% (multiplier: 0.16)
- Security Analyst: 10% (multiplier: 0.10)

## 3. Approval & Payroll Notification Protocol
1. Calculate the final bonus amount: `Annual Base Salary * Department Performance Multiplier`.
2. All finalized bonus allocations must be submitted via email to the Payroll Operations team at `finance.payroll@enterprise.io`.
3. The email notification must include:
   - Employee Full Name and Department
   - Annual Base Salary
   - Eligible Bonus Multiplier Percentage
   - Final Calculated Bonus Dollar Amount
