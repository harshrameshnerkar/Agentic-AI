2. DAY-BY-DAY DEVELOPMENT TIMELINE (DAYS 1 TO 5)

DAY 1: DATA COLLECTION, FIELD SPECIFICATION & GROUND TRUTH CREATION
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Build the evaluation benchmark FIRST before writing extraction code.
- Assembled 70 real commercial invoice documents in `data/documents/` representing realistic corporate variability:
  * 25 clean digital vector PDFs (SuperStore invoices with standard font streams).
  * 10 scanned raster PDFs with simulated scanner blur, contrast degradation, and slight skew.
  * 5 mobile photos / receipts in PNG and JPG formats.
  * 30 holdout and stress invoices (including `doc_069_corrupt.pdf` and `doc_070_blank.pdf`).
- Authored `docs/field_specification.md` defining the formal contract across 11 target fields:
  1. `invoice_id` (string, required): Unique document number. Strip prefix '#', 'Inv:', whitespace.
  2. `invoice_date` (ISO date YYYY-MM-DD, required): Document issuance date. Canonical ISO 8601 format.
  3. `due_date` (ISO date YYYY-MM-DD, nullable): Payment deadline. Missing dates return null; never synthesize.
  4. `vendor_name` (string, required): Legal billing entity name.
  5. `vendor_tax_id` (string, nullable): Tax/VAT/EIN identifier. Null if not printed.
  6. `customer_name` (string, required): Recipient customer/buyer name.
  7. `currency` (ISO 4217 enum, required): Standardized 3-letter currency code (USD, EUR, GBP, CAD, AUD, etc.).
  8. `subtotal` (Decimal, required): Pre-tax line item sum, 2 decimal places.
  9. `tax_amount` (Decimal, required): Total tax/VAT levied (0.00 if tax-exempt).
  10. `total_amount` (Decimal, required): Final payable amount. Must satisfy: subtotal + tax = total.
  11. `payment_terms` (Enum, required): NET_15, NET_30, NET_45, NET_60, DUE_ON_RECEIPT, UNKNOWN.
- Created `data/ground_truth/ground_truth.csv` containing 40 hand-keyed, verified records. Kept strictly separate
  from pipeline predictions. Validated accounting plausibility ($0.2 * subtotal <= total <= 2.5 * subtotal$)
  and vendor diversity (>= 10 unique vendors, balanced tax presence).

DAY 2: MULTIMODAL DOCUMENT LOADER & STRICT SCHEMA ENFORCEMENT
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Build a zero-crash document ingestion system and a strict typed Pydantic V2 schema.
- Built `src/loader.py`:
  * Employs `pdfplumber` to extract digital vector text streams when font tables are present (> 40 chars).
  * Falls back to `pypdfium2` at 200 DPI to render raster bitmaps for scanned pages and images.
  * Implements `_is_image_blank()` using pixel histogram analysis to detect blank pages without crashing.
  * Wraps corrupted files (`doc_069_corrupt.pdf`) in a `DocumentContent` object with `is_valid=False` and
    diagnostic error messages, guaranteeing the loader never throws unhandled fatal exceptions.
- Built `src/ocr.py`:
  * Wraps `pytesseract` with automatic binary path discovery across Windows, Linux, and macOS.
  * Provides OCR text extraction with `--psm 6` for tabular invoice layouts.
- Built `src/schemas.py`:
  * Enforces `InvoiceExtraction` using Pydantic V2.
  * Field validators normalize dates (`parse_flexible_date`), decimals (`parse_flexible_decimal` handles both
    US '$1,200.50' and European '1.200,50 €' notation), strip tax prefixes, and map fuzzy terms to Enums.
  * Rejects impossible dates (e.g. '2024-02-30') with a `pydantic.ValidationError`.
- Built `src/validator.py`:
  * Decouples validation from extraction: `InvoiceValidator` checks types, critical anchor constraints,
    arithmetic coherence ($|subtotal + tax - total| <= 0.02$), chronological validity ($invoice_date <= due_date$),
    and non-negative monetary rules. Returns structured feedback for self-healing retries.
- Built `src/extractor.py`:
  * `InvoiceExtractor` orchestrates Gemini multimodal vision calls with an automated retry loop (max 3 retries).
  * Injects specific validator diagnostic errors into retry prompts.
  * Integrates persistent offline caching (`data/extractions_cache.json`) for speed and cost efficiency.

DAY 3: FIELD-LEVEL ACCURACY BENCHMARK & WEAKEST FIELD DIAGNOSTICS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Benchmark predictions against hand-keyed ground truth across all 11 fields.
- Built `src/evaluator.py` featuring domain-specific comparison rules (`FieldMatcher`):
  * `match_invoice_id`: Exact string match after stripping symbols ('#', 'Inv:') and leading zeros.
  * `match_date`: Canonical ISO 8601 date string comparison (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
  * `match_fuzzy_name`: RapidFuzz `token_set_ratio >= 85.0%` (handles legal entity variations like "LLC" vs "Inc").
  * `match_tax_id`: Exact alphanumeric match ignoring spaces and dashes.
  * `match_amount`: Strict absolute monetary epsilon difference $|pred - truth| <= 0.02$.
  * `match_enum`: Case-insensitive standardized enum equality.
- Executed evaluation across all 40 ground truth documents (440 individual field instances).
- Generated `outputs/accuracy_report.csv` delivering **94.8% overall field accuracy**:
  * 100.0% accuracy on `invoice_id`, `invoice_date`, `vendor_name`, `customer_name`, `subtotal`, `tax_amount`, `total_amount`.
  * 92.5% accuracy on `currency`.
  * 90.0% accuracy on `vendor_tax_id`.
  * 80.0% accuracy on `due_date` (Weakest Field #1).
  * 80.0% accuracy on `payment_terms` (Weakest Field #2).
- Documented root causes and mitigations in `docs/results_report.md`:
  * `due_date` failures occur because B2B invoices frequently print credit terms rather than calendar dates; our specification
    forbids the model from synthesizing assumptions.
  * `payment_terms` failures stem from diverse natural language phrasing; mitigated with fuzzy enum parsing.

DAY 4: MULTI-FACTOR CONFIDENCE SCORING & HUMAN REVIEW QUEUE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Replace unreliable LLM self-reported confidence with deterministic multi-factor scoring.
- Built `src/confidence.py` implementing `ConfidenceEngine`:
  * Computes a composite confidence score in $[0.0, 1.0]$ across 4 independent evidence factors:
    1. Critical Anchor Presence (Weight: 0.35): `invoice_id`, `invoice_date`, `total_amount`. Missing any anchor
       drops anchor score to 0.0 and strictly blocks auto-approval.
    2. Arithmetic Coherence (Weight: 0.30): $|subtotal + tax - total| <= 0.02$. A mismatch incurs a -0.20 penalty
       and logs an arithmetic violation reason.
    3. Source Fidelity (Weight: 0.20): Vector PDF (0.20), clean scan (0.16), photo (0.14), corrupt/blank (0.00).
    4. Extraction Stability (Weight: 0.15): Full score for 0 retries; -0.05 penalty per retry iteration.
- Conducted empirical threshold sweep ($\theta \in [0.60, 0.95]$) documented in `docs/results_report.md`:
  * At $\theta = 0.80$, the pipeline achieves **85.0% Straight-Through Auto-Approval** (34/40 docs) with
    **0.0% Escaped Critical Errors**.
  * Documents with missing anchors or math mismatches (e.g. `doc_026.pdf` to `doc_031.pdf`) are routed to human review.
- Built `src/review_queue.py`:
  * `HumanReviewQueue` manages flagged items, tracks status (`PENDING_REVIEW`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, `OVERRIDDEN`),
    and exports an audit trail to `outputs/review_queue.csv`.

DAY 5: FAULT-TOLERANT BATCH PIPELINE, UNIT ECONOMICS & HANDOVER UI
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Objective: Build a crash-resilient batch pipeline, model 10k-document enterprise economics, and deliver an interactive UI.
- Built `src/batch_runner.py`:
  * `BatchPipelineRunner` processes directory batches sequentially with atomic state checkpointing (`outputs/batch_checkpoint.json`).
  * If the pipeline is interrupted or encounters corrupt files (`doc_069_corrupt.pdf`), it logs the failure to `outputs/failures.jsonl`,
    saves progress, and resumes seamlessly without reprocessing completed documents.
  * Emits clean deliverables: `predictions.jsonl`, `failures.jsonl`, `review_queue.csv`, and `batch_summary.json`.
- Built `src/cost_tracker.py`:
  * Measures token usage: 718 input tokens, 141 output tokens per document $\to$ **$0.000128 per document** ($0.128 per 1,000 docs).
  * Models monthly operational costs for 10,000 invoices:
    * 100% Manual Entry Baseline: 583.3 hours at $25/hr = $14,582.50 / month.
    * Automated HITL Pipeline: $1.28 API tokens + $6.00 hosting + 37.5 hours review labor ($937.50) = **$944.78 / month**.
    * **Net Monthly Savings: $13,637.72 (93.5% Cost Reduction) $\to$ $163,652.64 annual savings.**
- Built `app.py`:
  * Interactive Streamlit Review Center featuring:
    * Tab 1: Side-by-Side Review (rendered PDF image on the left, editable fields with confidence badges on the right).
    * Tab 2: Telemetry & Accuracy Dashboard (KPI cards, 11-field accuracy table, audit log).
    * Tab 3: Unit Economics & ROI Modeler (real-time interactive sliders for volume, hourly rate, and savings).
- Built comprehensive test suite in `tests/`: 42 tests across 6 files, 100% passing.

3.1 src/loader.py — Multimodal Document Loader & Zero-Crash Containment
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Universal document ingestion for vector PDFs, scanned raster PDFs, and mobile images with zero-crash guarantees.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `DocumentContent` (dataclass):
  * `doc_id: str`: Unique document identifier (e.g. 'doc_001').
  * `filename: str`: File basename on disk.
  * `file_path: Path`: Absolute path to source document.
  * `doc_type: str`: Detected format ('pdf_text', 'pdf_scanned', 'image_png', 'image_jpeg', 'corrupt', 'blank').
  * `is_valid: bool`: Flag indicating whether file can be parsed.
  * `error_message: Optional[str]`: Failure diagnostic if unreadable.
  * `text_content: Optional[str]`: Extracted digital text or OCR text.
  * `image_base64: Optional[str]`: Base64-encoded PNG string for multimodal LLM vision.
  * `page_count: int`: Number of pages in document.
  * `has_text: bool`: Helper property (True if text length > 20).
  * `has_image: bool`: Helper property (True if image_base64 is present).
  * `get_summary() -> str`: Formatted status string.

- `DocumentLoader`:
  * `__init__(render_scale: float = 2.0)`: Initializes loader with PDF rasterization scale (2.0 = ~200 DPI).
  * `load_document(file_path: Union[str, Path]) -> DocumentContent`:
    - Validates file existence on disk.
    - Routes `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg` to `_load_image()`.
    - Routes `.pdf` to `_load_pdf()`.
    - Catches all OS/IO exceptions and returns `DocumentContent(is_valid=False, doc_type="corrupt")`.
  * `_load_image(path: Path, doc_id: str, filename: str, suffix: str) -> DocumentContent`:
    - Verifies image integrity via `PIL.Image.open()` and `img.verify()`.
    - Converts RGB and encodes to Base64 data string.
  * `_load_pdf(path: Path, doc_id: str, filename: str) -> DocumentContent`:
    - Checks file size (0 bytes $\to$ blank/corrupt).
    - Attempts text extraction via `pdfplumber`. If text > 40 chars, classifies as `'pdf_text'`.
    - If text <= 40 chars, invokes `pypdfium2` to render page 0 bitmap at 200 DPI.
    - Evaluates `_is_image_blank()`. If blank, marks `'blank'`. Otherwise marks `'pdf_scanned'`.
  * `_is_image_blank(img: Image.Image, threshold: float = 0.995) -> bool`:
    - Converts image to grayscale, computes histogram, and checks if dominant pixel ratio exceeds 99.5%.

3.2 src/ocr.py — Tesseract OCR Fallback & High-Res PDF Page Rasterizer
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Standalone OCR processing and PDF page rendering for scanned or image-based invoices.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `OCRProcessor`:
  * `__init__(tesseract_cmd: Optional[str] = None)`:
    - Auto-discovers Tesseract binary on Windows (`C:\Program Files\Tesseract-OCR\tesseract.exe`,
      `C:\Users\<user>\AppData\Local\Programs\Tesseract-OCR\tesseract.exe`) and Linux/macOS PATH.
    - Disables gracefully if binary is not installed, falling back to direct vision encoding.
  * `extract_text_from_image(image_input: Union[Image.Image, bytes, Path]) -> str`:
    - Executes `pytesseract.image_to_string(image, config='--psm 6')` for uniform blocks of text.
    - Catches OCR execution errors and returns an empty string rather than crashing.
  * `render_pdf_to_images(pdf_path: Path, dpi: int = 200) -> List[Image.Image]`:
    - Uses `pypdfium2.PdfDocument` to render all pages into high-resolution PIL Images.

3.3 src/schemas.py — Pydantic V2 Strict Contract, Types & Value Normalizers
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Strict data contract enforcing types, constraints, and standard formats for commercial invoice extractions.

Key Enums & Classes:
- `CurrencyEnum(str, Enum)`:
  * Supported: `'USD'`, `'EUR'`, `'GBP'`, `'CAD'`, `'AUD'`, `'INR'`, `'JPY'`, `'CHF'`.
- `PaymentTermsEnum(str, Enum)`:
  * Supported: `'NET_15'`, `'NET_30'`, `'NET_45'`, `'NET_60'`, `'DUE_ON_RECEIPT'`, `'UNKNOWN'`.
- `InvoiceExtraction(BaseModel)`:
  * Fields:
    - `invoice_id: Optional[str] = None`
    - `invoice_date: Optional[date] = None`
    - `due_date: Optional[date] = None`
    - `vendor_name: Optional[str] = None`
    - `vendor_tax_id: Optional[str] = None`
    - `customer_name: Optional[str] = None`
    - `currency: Optional[CurrencyEnum] = CurrencyEnum.USD`
    - `subtotal: Optional[Decimal] = None`
    - `tax_amount: Optional[Decimal] = None`
    - `total_amount: Optional[Decimal] = None`
    - `payment_terms: PaymentTermsEnum = PaymentTermsEnum.UNKNOWN`

Field Normalizers & Validators:
- `clean_invoice_id(cls, v)`: Strips `#`, `Invoice:`, `Inv No:`, and extraneous whitespace.
- `validate_dates(cls, v)`: Calls `parse_flexible_date()`:
  * Handles ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`, European `DD/MM/YYYY`, US `MM/DD/YYYY`, and textual dates ("October 21, 2026").
  * Rejects calendar-impossible dates (`2024-02-30`) with `ValueError` (raising `ValidationError`).
- `validate_decimals(cls, v)`: Calls `parse_flexible_decimal()`:
  * Handles currency symbols (`$`, `€`, `£`, `¥`), spaces, and European decimal commas (`1.250,50` $\to$ `1250.50`).
  * Quantizes to 2 decimal places (`Decimal('0.01')`).
- `validate_currency(cls, v)`: Converts symbols (`$`, `€`, `£`, `C$`) and lowercase strings to `CurrencyEnum`.
- `validate_payment_terms(cls, v)`: Normalizes natural language phrases ("Net 30 days", "Due upon receipt") to Enum.
- `clean_tax_id(cls, v)`: Strips prefixes like `VAT:`, `Tax ID:`, `EIN:`.
- `to_ground_truth_dict() -> Dict[str, Any]`: Formats dates to ISO strings, Decimals to floats for evaluation.

3.4 src/validator.py — Separated Validation Rules & Business Accounting Logic
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Evaluates candidate extraction dictionaries against constraints and accounting rules independently from extraction.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `ValidationResult` (dataclass):
  * `is_valid: bool`: Overall validity flag.
  * `errors: List[str]`: Critical violations blocking approval.
  * `warnings: List[str]`: Non-critical alerts (e.g. missing optional fields).
  * `field_diagnostics: Dict[str, str]`: Field-level error messages.
  * `is_retryable: bool`: True if errors can be corrected via LLM prompt refinement.
  * `error_prompt_feedback: str`: Formatted feedback string injected into LLM retry prompts.

- `InvoiceValidator`:
  * `validate_candidate(candidate: Dict[str, Any]) -> ValidationResult`:
    1. Critical Anchor Check: Verifies presence of `invoice_id`, `invoice_date`, and `total_amount`.
    2. Arithmetic Coherence Check: Verifies $|subtotal + tax - total| <= 0.02$.
       - If subtotal and tax are provided, total MUST equal their sum within 2 cents tolerance.
    3. Chronological Coherence Check: Verifies $invoice_date <= due_date$.
    4. Non-Negative Monetary Check: Ensures `subtotal`, `tax_amount`, and `total_amount` are >= 0.00.
    5. Schema Compatibility Check: Runs raw candidate through `InvoiceExtraction` to verify type coercibility.

3.5 src/extractor.py — Multimodal LLM Client, Self-Healing Retries & Persistent Cache
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Coordinates document extraction via Gemini multimodal API with validation retry loops and caching.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `ExtractionMetadata` (dataclass):
  * `retries_used: int`: Number of retry attempts required.
  * `validation_errors: List[str]`: List of errors encountered during validation.
  * `latency_ms: float`: Execution duration in milliseconds.
  * `input_tokens: int`: Prompt token count.
  * `output_tokens: int`: Completion token count.
  * `source_type: str`: 'vector_text', 'scanned_image', or 'corrupt'.
  * `cached: bool`: True if served from local cache.

- `InvoiceExtractor`:
  * `__init__(cache_path=None, max_retries=3, model=None, api_key=None)`:
    - Loads persistent cache from `data/extractions_cache.json`.
    - Initializes Google GenAI client (`gemini-2.5-flash` or `gemini-1.5-flash`).
  * `extract(doc: DocumentContent, force_fresh: bool = False) -> Tuple[InvoiceExtraction, ExtractionMetadata]`:
    - Fast exit for invalid documents (corrupt/blank): returns empty schema with metadata.
    - Cache lookup: if `doc.doc_id` exists in cache and `not force_fresh`, returns cached extraction instantly.
    - Builds multimodal extraction prompt containing formal 11-field specification.
    - Enters self-healing retry loop:
      * Calls Gemini API with document text or Base64 image.
      * Strips markdown fences (```json ... ```) and parses candidate JSON.
      * Passes candidate to `InvoiceValidator.validate_candidate()`.
      * If valid $\to$ constructs `InvoiceExtraction`, saves to cache, returns result.
      * If invalid $\to$ appends `validation_result.error_prompt_feedback` to prompt and retries.
    - If retries exhausted, returns best-effort extraction with validation failure warnings logged.

3.6 src/evaluator.py — Field-Level Accuracy Evaluator & Specialized Matching Engine
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Evaluates predictions against hand-keyed ground truth across 11 fields using specialized matching logic.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `FieldMatcher`:
  * `is_empty(val: Any) -> bool`: Checks if value is None, empty string, or 'null'.
  * `match_invoice_id(pred, truth) -> bool`: Exact match after stripping `#`, spaces, leading zeros.
  * `match_date(pred, truth) -> bool`: Normalizes dates to ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` and asserts exact equality.
  * `match_fuzzy_name(pred, truth, threshold=85.0) -> bool`: Computes `RapidFuzz` token set ratio.
  * `match_tax_id(pred, truth) -> bool`: Compares alphanumeric characters ignoring formatting.
  * `match_amount(pred, truth, epsilon=0.02) -> bool`: Asserts $|pred - truth| <= 0.02$.
  * `match_enum(pred, truth) -> bool`: Case-insensitive standardized string comparison.
  * `match_field(field_name, pred_val, truth_val) -> bool`: Dispatches field to appropriate matcher.
  * `evaluate_document(predicted: Dict, ground_truth: Dict) -> Dict[str, bool]`: Scores all 11 fields.

- `FieldAccuracyMetric` (dataclass):
  * `field_name: str`, `total_evaluated: int`, `correct_count: int`, `accuracy_pct: float`, `weakest_rank: Optional[int]`.

- `EvaluationResults` (dataclass):
  * `total_documents: int`, `total_fields_evaluated: int`, `overall_accuracy_pct: float`,
    `field_metrics: Dict[str, FieldAccuracyMetric]`, `weakest_fields: List[str]`, `per_document_scores: Dict[str, float]`.

- `FieldEvaluator`:
  * `__init__(ground_truth_path="data/ground_truth/ground_truth.csv", predictions_path="outputs/predictions.jsonl")`:
    - Strict rule: Keeps ground truth separate from predictions; never derives ground truth from model output.
  * `run_evaluation() -> EvaluationResults`:
    - Iterates over all 40 ground truth records, matches against predictions, and compiles accuracy table.
  * `export_accuracy_report(out_csv_path="outputs/accuracy_report.csv")`:
    - Exports per-field accuracy benchmark CSV.

3.7 src/confidence.py — Deterministic Multi-Factor Confidence Engine & Threshold Sweep
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Purpose: Computes composite confidence scores from deterministic signals and routes ambiguous documents to human reviewers.

Key Classes & Functions:
- `ConfidenceReport` (dataclass):
  * `composite_score: float`: Combined score in range $[0.0, 1.0]$.
  * `is_auto_approved: bool`: True if score >= threshold AND critical anchors present AND math coherent.
  * `anchor_score: float`: Factor score for critical anchors (0.0 to 0.35).
  * `math_score: float`: Factor score for arithmetic coherence (0.0 to 0.30).
  * `fidelity_score: float`: Factor score for document input quality (0.0 to 0.20).
  * `stability_score: float`: Factor score for extraction retries (0.0 to 0.15).
  * `review_reasons: List[str]`: Detailed reasons if routed to review queue.
  * `field_scores: Dict[str, float]`: Individual field confidence scores.

- `ConfidenceEngine`:
  * `DEFAULT_THRESHOLD = 0.80`: Empirically validated optimal operating point.
  * `compute_confidence(doc, extraction, metadata=None, custom_threshold=None) -> ConfidenceReport`:
    1. Critical Anchor Check (Weight: 0.35): Checks `invoice_id`, `invoice_date`, `total_amount`.
       - If any anchor is missing, anchor score = 0.0 and auto-approval is strictly BLOCKED.
    2. Arithmetic Coherence Check (Weight: 0.30): Computes $|subtotal + tax - total|$.
       - If diff <= 0.02 $\to$ math score = 0.30.
       - If diff > 0.02 $\to$ math score = 0.10, logs arithmetic violation, and BLOCKS auto-approval.
    3. Source Fidelity Check (Weight: 0.20):
       - Vector text: 0.20; Clean scan: 0.16; Mobile photo: 0.14; Corrupt/Blank: 0.00.
    4. Extraction Stability Check (Weight: 0.15):
       - 0 retries: 0.15; 1 retry: 0.10; 2 retries: 0.05; >= 3 retries: 0.00.
    - Auto-Approval Rule: `composite_score >= threshold` AND `len(review_reasons) == 0`.

- `tune_threshold_sweep(ground_truth_records, predictions)`:
  * Evaluates candidate thresholds $\theta \in [0.60, 0.65, 0.70, 0.75, 0.80, 0.85, 0.90, 0.95]$.
  * Demonstrates monotonic auto-approval decrease and validates $\theta = 0.80$ as the optimal trade-off:
    85.0% auto-approval, 0.0% escaped critical errors.

6. PIPELINE RUNTIME EXECUTION FLOW (END-TO-END STEP-BY-STEP)
========================================================================================================================

When running the batch pipeline (`python week5/src/batch_runner.py`), the execution progresses through these stages:

STAGE 1: DOCUMENT DISCOVERY & INITIALIZATION
- Discovers all supported files (`.pdf`, `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`) in `week5/data/documents/`.
- Initializes `outputs/` directory structure.
- Checks for existing `outputs/batch_checkpoint.json`. If present and `resume=True`, loads completed doc IDs.

STAGE 2: MULTIMODAL INGESTION (`src/loader.py`)
- Loops through discovered document paths.
- For each file:
  * Calls `DocumentLoader.load_document(path)`.
  * If file is binary corrupted (`doc_069_corrupt.pdf`):
    - Marks `is_valid = False`, `doc_type = "corrupt"`.
    - Logs error record to `outputs/failures.jsonl`.
    - Checkpoints failure state to `outputs/batch_checkpoint.json`.
    - Continues to next document without halting the pipeline.
  * If file is blank (`doc_070_blank.pdf`):
    - Identifies dominant pixel ratio > 99.5%, marks `doc_type = "blank"`.
    - Flags for human review with reason "Blank document detected".
  * If file is digital vector PDF (`doc_001.pdf` to `doc_025.pdf`):
    - Extracts font stream text via `pdfplumber`. Sets `doc_type = "pdf_text"`.
  * If file is scanned PDF (`doc_026.pdf` to `doc_035.pdf`):
    - Renders page 0 bitmap at 200 DPI via `pypdfium2`. Base64 encodes image. Sets `doc_type = "pdf_scanned"`.
  * If file is raster image (`doc_036.png` to `doc_040.jpg`):
    - Verifies PIL image integrity, encodes to Base64. Sets `doc_type = "image_png"` / `"image_jpeg"`.

STAGE 3: EXTRACTION & VALIDATION RETRY LOOP (`src/extractor.py`, `src/validator.py`)
- Checks offline cache (`data/extractions_cache.json`).
  * If cached: loads extraction candidate in < 5 ms.
  * If fresh: submits prompt + text/image to Gemini multimodal API (`gemini-2.5-flash`).
- Passes raw candidate dictionary to `InvoiceValidator.validate_candidate()`:
  * Validates required anchors (`invoice_id`, `invoice_date`, `total_amount`).
  * Validates arithmetic ($|subtotal + tax - total| <= 0.02$).
  * Validates chronology ($invoice_date <= due_date$).
  * If validation fails and retries < 3:
    - Injects `error_prompt_feedback` into retry prompt.
    - Re-prompts Gemini to correct specific errors.
  * Instantiates strict Pydantic V2 model `InvoiceExtraction(**candidate)`.
  * Saves successful extraction to cache.

STAGE 4: MULTI-FACTOR CONFIDENCE SCORING & HITL ROUTING (`src/confidence.py`, `src/review_queue.py`)
- Evaluates extracted fields via `ConfidenceEngine.compute_confidence()`:
  * Computes Anchor Score ($w = 0.35$).
  * Computes Math Score ($w = 0.30$).
  * Computes Source Fidelity Score ($w = 0.20$).
  * Computes Extraction Stability Score ($w = 0.15$).
  * Determines Composite Score $\in [0.0, 1.0]$.
- Evaluates against optimal threshold $\theta = 0.80$:
  * If score >= 0.80 AND all anchors present AND math coherent:
    - Status: `AUTO_APPROVED` (Straight-Through Processing).
    - Appends record to `outputs/predictions.jsonl`.
  * If score < 0.80 OR missing anchor OR math mismatch:
    - Status: `NEEDS_REVIEW`.
    - Appends record to `outputs/predictions.jsonl`.
    - Enqueues document in `HumanReviewQueue`.
    - Updates `outputs/review_queue.csv` with triggered review reasons.

STAGE 5: ATOMIC CHECKPOINTING & SUMMARY EXPORT (`src/batch_runner.py`)
- After processing each document, writes current batch state to `outputs/batch_checkpoint.json`.
- Upon batch completion:
  * Compiles `BatchExecutionSummary`:
    - Total Documents: 40 ground truth docs (or 70 full corpus).
    - Auto-Approval Rate: 85.0%.
    - Escaped Critical Error Rate: 0.0%.
    - Crash Survival Rate: 100.0%.
  * Writes final KPI report to `outputs/batch_summary.json`.

STAGE 6: FIELD-LEVEL ACCURACY BENCHMARKING (`src/evaluator.py`)
- Runs `FieldEvaluator.run_evaluation()` comparing `outputs/predictions.jsonl` against `data/ground_truth/ground_truth.csv`.
- Executes specialized matching rules per field.
- Generates `outputs/accuracy_report.csv` delivering **94.8% overall field accuracy**.

9. WHAT WE TRIED THAT DID NOT WORK (ENGINEERING POST-MORTEM)
========================================================================================================================

In strict accordance with Rule 4 of the assignment brief ("Show what didn't work"), here are the real engineering
approaches that were implemented, empirically tested, and rejected during development:

1. LLM SELF-REPORTED CONFIDENCE ("confidence": 0.0 - 1.0)
   - Hypothesis: Prompting the vision model to evaluate its own certainty and return a confidence score (or token logprobs)
     would provide a reliable signal to route low-quality documents to human review.
   - Why It Failed: Severe overconfidence hallucination. On low-resolution, noisy scans where vendor tax IDs were OCR-mangled,
     the model returned confidence scores of 0.98 for completely hallucinated strings. A self-evaluating model cannot detect
     what it cannot see.
   - The Fix: Completely eliminated LLM self-confidence. Built a deterministic **Evidence-Based Multi-Factor Confidence Engine**
     (`ConfidenceEngine` in `src/confidence.py`) combining:
     * Critical anchor presence (weight 0.35; missing anchor caps score below 0.80).
     * Mathematical coherence ($|subtotal + tax - total| <= 0.02$) (weight 0.30; mismatch incurs -0.20 penalty).
     * Source document fidelity (vector text 0.20 vs photo 0.14).
     * Extraction stability (-0.05 penalty per retry iteration).

2. PRE-EXTRACTION REGEX FILTERING FOR DATES AND AMOUNTS
   - Hypothesis: Running regex patterns on raw OCR text before invoking the LLM to extract dates and total amounts, aiming
     to reduce prompt tokens and inference latency.
   - Why It Failed: Commercial invoices routinely contain 4 to 8 competing dates (Issue Date, Order Date, Ship Date, Due Date,
     Tax Point Date) and dozens of monetary amounts (Unit Price, Discount, Line Subtotal, Tax Rate, Total Due). Regex lacked
     spatial and semantic context, frequently mapping Order Date to Invoice Date and grabbing line-item totals instead of the
     binding balance due.
   - The Fix: Preserved multimodal LLM extraction for semantic disambiguation, but enforced rigid downstream schema validation
     (`src/validator.py`) and programmatic math checking to guarantee precision.

3. IN-CONTEXT CHAIN-OF-THOUGHT (CoT) CALCULATION OF MISSING DUE DATES
   - Hypothesis: When an invoice printed "Terms: Net 30" but omitted an explicit calendar due date, we prompted the model
     to calculate `invoice_date + 30 days` to fill in the field.
   - Why It Failed: Introduced two critical failures:
     1. Date arithmetic hallucination around month boundaries and leap years (e.g. producing impossible dates like 2024-02-30).
     2. Violation of extraction fidelity: extracting document facts vs. synthesizing business assumptions.
   - The Fix: Codified in `docs/field_specification.md` that missing due dates must return `null`. Downstream ERP accounting
     systems apply deterministic payment term logic; the vision/extraction pipeline must never invent dates.
